📖 Water-Borne Diseases in Northeast India

⚠️
Cholera

Caused by contaminated water. Symptoms: severe diarrhea, vomiting, dehydration.

⚠️
Typhoid

Spread through contaminated water/food. Symptoms: high fever, weakness, stomach pain.

⚠️
Dysentery

Intestinal infection from dirty water. Symptoms: diarrhea with blood, fever, cramps.
